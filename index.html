how it began

Our Story

12 years, two countries with different time zones, and one very persistent golden retriever later, here we are — writing the last chapter as two and the first as one.

Aug 2013 · Kolhapur

Where we met

Our story started at diploma college — just two classmates sitting a few benches apart, passing notes, sharing group projects, & slowly becoming inseparable friends. We laughed through lectures, helped each other through deadlines, & made memories that still make us smile.

Apr 2014 · Over a call

The proposal

And the funniest part? Nobody ever officially proposed! 😂 No grand speeches, no dramatic gestures — we just looked at each other one day and knew: this is it. We were already writing our forever, one beautiful moment at a time.

Sep 2014 · Army Campus

Our first date

Time passed, laughter grew, and love quietly found its way. A year later, we went on our first date. But the moment we’ll never forget? That magical day at Army Campus.

Aug 2016 ~ May 2025

In A Long Distance Relationship

Nine years of long-distance love across two cities, two countries, and different time zones — filled with late-night calls, patience, trust, and a few fights. Through it all, we chased dreams, supported each other, and grew stronger together. Every mile brought us closer to the life we always dreamed of — together.

Jan 2027 · Wedding

Forever begins

Now, after 12 beautiful years of love, friendship, laughter, and countless memories, our hearts have found their forever. From dreaming together to making those dreams come true, we can finally say, “We did it!” We’re ready to turn our love story into a lifetime promise. With your love, blessings, and warm wishes, join Kumar and Prajakta as we begin our forever — hand in hand, heart to heart.
